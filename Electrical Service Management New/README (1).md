# ⚡ Electrical Service Management System

A modern web-based **Electrical Service Management System** designed to manage customers, electrical services, billing, payments, and service history efficiently.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Electrical Service Management System helps electricians and service providers manage their daily operations digitally.

It provides features like:

- Customer Management
- Service Management
- Bill Generation
- Payment Tracking
- Service History
- Admin Dashboard
- Firebase Database Integration

---

## 🚀 Features

### 👤 Customer Management
- Add new customers
- Store customer details
- View customer records
- Update customer information

### 🔧 Service Management
- Add electrical services
- Manage service categories
- Track service requests

### 🧾 Billing System
- Generate invoices
- Calculate service charges
- Save billing history

### 💳 Payment Management
- Track payments
- QR payment support
- Payment status management

### 📊 Dashboard
- Total customers
- Total services
- Total bills
- Revenue overview

### 📜 History
- View previous services
- Check billing records
